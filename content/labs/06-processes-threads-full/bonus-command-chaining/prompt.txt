Bonus (b): the shell chaining commands with the ;, && and || operators.

* Goal: exit-status parsing and the list operators.
* Description: Extend the background shell so several commands go on one line,
  joined by ;, && and ||, each deciding from the previous status whether the
  next runs.  No I/O redirection or piping.

Builds on bonus-background-processes (cumulative): arguments, cd/exit
builtins, background `&` and reaping are already present.  The tokenizer
(splitting at spaces, tabs and ;), is_operator() and the cutting loop are
given; the student writes the status unpacking in run_command() and the
run-or-skip + builtin dispatch in run_line().
