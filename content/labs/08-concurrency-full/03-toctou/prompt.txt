6. An exercise as a challenge with a simple TOCTOU problem.

A registry of names with registry_contains() and a static registry_add(),
each correctly locked, and registry_register() written as "if not
contains, add".  The bug is the gap between the two critical sections.
The story is deliberately not the ticket sale of the lecture's toctou
demo.  The executable lines the threads up at a barrier before every
name, so that the window is hit on every run.  A lock around the two
calls, with the same mutex, deadlocks a thread against itself; that trap
is intended.
