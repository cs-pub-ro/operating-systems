11. A bonus exercise as a challenge with a badly implemented boss-workers
    scenario.

boss_run(work, nitems, nworkers): the calling thread is the boss, the
library starts and joins the workers.  Between them is a desk for one
item, so that the hand-over stays small and the exercise is about the
end.  Three planted flaws: one condition variable shared by the boss and
the workers and woken with signal; the end announced with signal; and a
worker that leaves on `stop` without looking at the desk, which loses
the last item in some rounds.
