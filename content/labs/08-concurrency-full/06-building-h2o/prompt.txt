9. An exercise as a challenge showing a badly implemented "Building H2O"
   synchronization problem.

hydrogen() and oxygen() in the library, each of which has to call the
executable's release_hydrogen() or release_oxygen() once.  The skeleton
has two semaphores, 2 and 1, around the release, which limit how many
atoms are released at once and do not group them.  The reference adds a
barrier of three and keeps the post after the release.

The threads of the executable share a counter of atoms to bring instead
of each bringing a fixed number: with fixed shares the last hydrogen
thread can be left without a partner, and a correct library hangs.
