12. A bonus exercise as a challenge with a badly implemented
    worker-threads scenario (as a build up of the previous bonus
    exercise).

A thread pool: pool_create(), pool_submit(), pool_wait(),
pool_destroy(), over a linked-list queue.  It builds on the boss and the
workers by keeping the threads alive between uses, letting any thread
submit, including a running task, and adding the question of when the
work is finished.  The executable's tasks split ranges and submit the
halves, so that "the queue is empty" and "the work is done" differ.

Three planted flaws: the worker runs the task with the pool's lock held
(a task that submits deadlocks against its own worker), pool_wait()
polls the head of the queue without the lock, and pool_destroy() repeats
the single signal of the previous exercise.
