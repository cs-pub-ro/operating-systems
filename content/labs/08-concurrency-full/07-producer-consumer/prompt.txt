10. An exercise as a challenge showing a badly implemented
    producer-consumer synchronization problem.

A bounded queue over a circular buffer, behind an opaque pointer, with
queue_put() and queue_get().  Two planted flaws, the two that the
condition-variable demo warned about: the waits use `if`, and producers
and consumers share one condition variable woken with signal.  The first
gives duplicated and lost items; with it fixed, the second gives a hang.
The capacity is 2, with four producers and four consumers, because with
a larger buffer the second flaw did not show.
