7. An exercise as a challenge with a simple deadlock problem.

Bank accounts with one mutex each, and a transfer that locks `from` and
then `to`.  The executable's threads go both ways around a ring of four
accounts, so two threads soon ask for the same pair in opposite orders.
The transfers are fixed in advance and no balance is checked before
money is moved, so the final balances do not depend on the interleaving.
The alarm of the executable turns the hang into a message; `./chall 0`
leaves the program stuck, for ps and GDB.
