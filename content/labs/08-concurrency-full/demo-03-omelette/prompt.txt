3. A demo as a challenge showing a badly implemented synchronization where
   one requires 3 eggs to create an omelette.  You have "egg creation"
   threads (and function) and an "omelette making" thread (and function).

The challenge format is the one the prompt gives for all challenges: a
prebuilt executable that runs functions of a shared library and prints an
encrypted flag if all went well; students get the executable, its source
without the key, the Makefile and the header of the library, and a
skeleton of the library.

Here the executable owns the basket and the threads; the library has
egg_create() and omelette_make(), and calls back basket_put_egg() and
basket_take_eggs(), which detect two threads inside and an omelette
started with fewer than three eggs.  Three planted bugs: the hens' side is
unlocked, the cook waits with `if`, and the eggs are taken after the
unlock.
