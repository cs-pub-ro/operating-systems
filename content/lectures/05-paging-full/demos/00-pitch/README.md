# Demo: Billions of Faults

Two demos for the pitch, both read straight from `/proc`.

| Demo | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [`page-faults/`](page-faults) | Ten billion page faults since boot, and a browser with 1.6 billion of its own. |
| [`total-rss/`](total-rss) | The `RSS` of all processes adds up to more than the memory in use. |

Both are numbers the rest of the lecture explains: the first in parts 02 and 03, the second in part 04.
