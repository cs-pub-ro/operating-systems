We are preparing contents for a class on Operating Systems, part of a 4-years bachelor study program in a technical university.
The class is part of the 2nd year, 1s semester.

We are preparing the materials for the lecture-01 (the first lecture): Software Stack
The materials will be of two types:
- Materials to be used / delivered live, under `content/lectures/01-software-stack-live/`
- Materials to be used before and after lecture, with full information, details and explanations, under content/lectures/01-software-stack-full/`

The live / full split follows the similar split used for the labs (the practical part of the class), as in `content/labs/01-software-stack-live/` and `contents/labs/1-software-stack-full/`.

The live delivery of the lecture will take 100 minutes (50 minutes, 10 minutes break, another 50 minutes). In actuality we can expect the delivery to be 40 minutes + 10 minutes break + 40 minutes, because of starting delays and concluding remarks.

Lecture materials, both live and full, are of three types:

- `README.md`: this is the reading material, serving as lesson plan for live and as extensive information for full
- `media/`: stores images and potentially, video, audio or other files as visual support
- `demos/`: practical demonstrations of a concept in action; they are to be demoed live in the live part (only minimal information) and followed individually by students in the full/ part
- `slides/`: the lectures slides:
   - for live: minimalistic, only with diagrams, references to demos and very relevant (yet minimal) information, shouldn't be more than 15 slides per lecture, following the plan in the `README.md`
   - for full: rich, with details, references / inclusion of diagrams, references for further reading, following the contents of the `README.md`

3. Create slides in `content/lectures/01-software-stack-full/slides/`.
   Use Quarto for slides (https://quarto.org/docs/presentations/).
